# MAS**CH**ERA

**English** · [Deutsch](README.de.md)

**Mask personal data in Swiss documents — on your own device, with no
network while it runs.** Four languages: German, French, Italian,
English.

You have a letter, an email thread or a PDF and want to ask an AI tool
about it — but the names, social security numbers and addresses should
stay behind. MASCHERA replaces them with placeholders:

    Sehr geehrte Frau Brülhart       ->   Sehr geehrte Frau [FULLNAME_1]
    AHV 756.1234.5678.97             ->   AHV [AHVN13_1]
    Lindenstrasse 12, 3011 Bern      ->   [STREET_1] [BUILDINGNUM_1], [ZIPCODE_1] [CITY_1]

You give the masked text to the service of your choice. Paste the answer
back, and MASCHERA puts the real values in again.

Made for everyone who works with Swiss documents: federal, cantonal and
municipal administrations, companies, law firms, associations and private
individuals.

**Detection runs entirely on your device.** While it runs, nothing is
downloaded and nothing is sent — no fonts, no icons, no analytics. There
is no endpoint that sends text anywhere, and there never will be; the way
out always goes through your clipboard.

⚠️ **The model is 1.2 GB, and there are two ways to ship it.** The
AppImage and the Windows installer carry it — download, start, done. The Flatpak fetches it **once, on first start**: from a
named source, with a checksum, and only after you agree. If you are not
on Wi-Fi right now, decline and fetch it later. **After that, both are
equally offline.**

*maschera* is Italian for **mask** — and carries the Swiss country code
**CH** in the middle.

- Website: [www.maschera.ch](https://www.maschera.ch)
- Source: [github.com/semprerudi/maschera](https://github.com/semprerudi/maschera)
- Model: [semprerudi/maschera-ch-v63b](https://huggingface.co/semprerudi/maschera-ch-v63b)
- Report a bug: [Issues](https://github.com/semprerudi/maschera/issues)

> ⚠️ **Pseudonymisation, not anonymisation.** As long as the dictionary
> exists, the process can be reversed — and under Swiss data protection
> law (revDSG) the data remain personal data. That is why the button says
> "Mask".

## How good is it?

The shipped model (`runs/ch-v63b`) was tested in two ways. **Both results
are here — including the less flattering one.**

| | practice texts (synthetic) | real documents (gold documents) |
|---|---|---|
| Size | 2000 generated texts, 29,210 personal details (gold spans) | 9 real documents, 280 personal details (gold spans) |
| **Left visible** (leak rate) | **0.02 %** — 13 details missed in whole or in part | **1.27 %** — 6 details missed in whole or in part |
| Overall score (micro-F1, 1 = perfect) | 0.997 | 0.736 |
| Masked too much | 84 places | 95 places |
| Anyone can recheck it | **yes** | **no** |

The **leak rate** is the share of letters and digits belonging to personal
details that can still be read after masking. The **overall score** sums
up how often MASCHERA finds the right places and how often it gets it
wrong.

**The practice texts** are generated by MASCHERA itself, from templates
and lists of names. Anyone with the source code gets exactly the same
numbers with this command:

```fish
python3 tools/mess_synthetisch.py --model runs/ch-v63b --n 2000
```

The numbers in the table were measured this way with MASCHERA 1.0.1. The
texts depend on the templates and the generator: if those change, the
numbers change with them, and the table has to be measured again.

⚠️ **The practice texts are familiar to the model.** They are built from
the same templates as the texts it learned from. It has never seen the
names and numbers in them, though — so it really has to recognise them
and cannot know them by heart. Real letters are still harder. That is why
there is a second test.

**The real documents** are nine letters in which every personal detail was
marked by hand. Because they contain real personal data, they are not
published — nobody can recheck this number. It is the most honest one,
though. How to build your own test documents: `docs/GOLDDOKUMENTE.md`
(German).

⚠️ **Nine documents are not many.** If two models differ by less than
about 0.06 in the overall score, that is chance with so few documents, not
an improvement.

**What this means for you:** in real documents a little over one percent
of personal details stays visible, not zero. MASCHERA takes the work off
your hands, not the responsibility — **look at the masked text before you
pass it on.** That is why area 02 shows every finding in colour, with
where it came from.

⚠️ **Masking too much is not a leak.** One word too many hidden makes the
text a little harder to read; one word too few gives away a personal
detail. When in doubt, MASCHERA hides too much.

## The model

`ch-v63b`, a fine-tuned **ModernBERT** (`jhu-clsp/mmBERT-base`), 22
layers, 308 M parameters. Trained on **synthetic** data generated from the
templates and nomenclatures in this repository — it has never seen a real
document.

The weights are on Hugging Face under **MIT**:
[semprerudi/maschera-ch-v63b](https://huggingface.co/semprerudi/maschera-ch-v63b).
They are not part of this repository — 1.2 GB does not belong in Git.

**The training path is open.** The training data is not shipped as a
file, it is GENERATED — templates, nomenclatures and generators are in
this repository:

```fish
python3 tools/generate_dataset.py --n 40000 --rauschen 0.08 \
        --zone training --seed 2026 --out dataset.jsonl
python3 tools/generate_dataset.py --n 2000 --rauschen 0.08 \
        --zone halten --seed 63 --out eval.jsonl
python3 train.py --data dataset.jsonl --eval-data eval.jsonl \
        --out runs/eigenes --max-length 512 --seed 4711
```

### What `ch-v63b` was trained with

Read from `training_args.bin` of the shipped run, not copied from the
defaults:

| | |
|---|---|
| Epochs | 3 |
| Learning rate | 3e-5, linear, warmup 0.1 |
| Batch | 8 × 2 (gradient accumulation) |
| Weight decay | 0.01 |
| Precision | bf16 |
| max_length | 512 |
| **Training seed** | **4711** |

⚠️ **`train.py` defaults to seed 2026**; the shipped run used **4711** —
without `--seed` you get a different model.

⚠️ **The seed of the TRAINING SET was not recorded.** `training_args.bin`
records how the model was trained, not on what. The command above
therefore uses 2026; that is a recommendation for rebuilding, **not a
statement about the shipped run**. The path can be walked; the exact
trail of that one run cannot. Future runs record both —
`generate_dataset.py` writes its seed into the header of the `.jsonl`.

⚠️ **`--zone` is not optional.** Without it, training and evaluation sets
draw from the same nomenclature, and the numbers would fit "recognises
names" and "memorised the list" equally well.

⚠️ **And the same seed is not enough.** If the generator changes, the
same command produces a different data set — the code version is part of
the recipe.

⚠️ **`--ohne-modell` (without model) is not an operating mode.** Without
the model only checksums and patterns apply, and every name gets
through. The command line allows it for measurements and says so.

## Install

Three ways on Linux, an installer for Windows. They differ in exactly one
question: **does the model ship with the package, or is it fetched on
first start?**

| | Package | Model | For whom |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AppImage** | about 1.7 GB | included | one executable file; no installation, no dependencies |
| **Flatpak** | about 0.3 GB, plus the KDE runtime from Flathub | fetched, 1.2 GB | Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, openSUSE, Arch — wherever Flatpak runs |
| **Windows installer** | about 1.4 GB | included | Windows 11, 64-bit; installs without administrator rights |
| **Docker** | about 4.2 GB | included | running as a service; interface in the browser |

If the model is fetched, **the first start asks first**: it names source,
size and destination, and "Later" closes the application without
downloading anything. After that everything runs offline.

AppImage and Flatpak bring their own libraries and do not depend on what
else is installed on the system.

### Download

The ready-made files are on the
[releases page](https://github.com/semprerudi/maschera/releases/latest).

```fish
chmod +x MASCHERA-latest-x86_64.AppImage
./MASCHERA-latest-x86_64.AppImage
```

```fish
flatpak install --user MASCHERA-latest-x86_64.flatpak
flatpak run ch.maschera.Maschera
```

The Flatpak fetches the KDE runtime from Flathub when installed.

On Windows, run `MASCHERA-latest-x64-setup.exe`. The installer is not
signed, so Windows warns on first start: "More info" → "Run anyway".

### Docker

A ready-made image, about 4.2 GB, the model included:

```fish
docker run -d --name maschera -p 127.0.0.1:4141:4141 \
    ghcr.io/semprerudi/maschera:1.0.1
```

Then open `http://127.0.0.1:4141` in the browser. The server keeps
nothing: no document, no dictionary, no log. To keep your own rules and
settings across restarts, add `-v maschera-config:/home/maschera/.config/maschera`.
Behind a reverse proxy or under another name than `127.0.0.1`, set
`MASCHERA_WIRT` — see `tools/paket/LIESMICH.md` (German).

### Build it yourself

```fish
fish tools/paket/appimage_bauen.fish          # AppImage
flatpak-builder --user --install \
    --state-dir bau/zustand bau/ziel \
    tools/paket/ch.maschera.Maschera.yml      # Flatpak
fish tools/paket/bauen.fish                   # Docker
```

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe tools\paket\windows_bauen.py   # Windows, on Windows
```

`tools/paket/LIESMICH.md` (German) describes each path and the decisions
behind it, including how to fetch the model for the AppImage and Docker
builds. `WINDOWS.md` (German) describes the Windows build as it was run;
`MACOS.md` is the script for macOS, which has not been run yet.

## Get started

```fish
python3 tools/run_tests.py
```

33 checks, no dependencies except PyYAML and Flask. No model, no GPU, no
downloads needed — **32 pass straight away in a fresh clone.**

The 33rd needs the model and says so; two more skip one point each as
long as the nomenclature has not been built
(`python3 packs/ch/nomenclatures/build.py`). Both show up in the output —
**a skipped check has checked nothing**, and the runner does not count it
as passed.

```fish
python3 tools/generate_dataset.py --n 20 --show
```

Generates training examples from the templates — shows what the result
looks like.

## Layout

```
packs/ch/          everything Swiss-specific
  taxonomy.yaml      45 tags, frozen BIO order  <- source of truth
  patterns.yaml      regexes with context anchors in four languages
  macros.yaml        coupled slot groups (@address, @person)
  pack.yaml          manifest, label contract hash, backbone
  adapter.py         the pack's interface
  validators/        checksums (AHV, UID, IBAN, QR, Luhn, GLN)
  generators/        generate valid identifiers
  nomenclatures/     build.py + data sources (data NOT in the repo)
  templates/         templates per language

core/              language-independent mechanics
  recognizers.py     regex + anchors + checksums
  masking.py         masking, dictionary, propagation
  injector.py        template + nomenclature -> training example
  alignment.py       character span <-> BIO (tokenizer-independent)
  evaluate.py        leak rate, per-entity metrics
  inference.py       the three stages combined

app/               interface and service
tools/             tools
  run_tests.py         all checks
  check_taxonomy.py    label contract
  generate_dataset.py  generate a training set
  verify_tokenizer.py  check tokenizer assumptions (needs transformers)
  mess_synthetisch.py  the reproducible measurement
  build_release.py     ONNX + INT8 (optional, not the shipping path)
  paket/               AppImage, Flatpak, Docker

site/              the project website (www.maschera.ch)

train.py           training (needs torch + GPU)
tests/             32 test files
```

## The one rule

The order in `taxonomy.yaml` is a contract. Append new tags, never
insert, never reorder. Otherwise old checkpoints silently map to the
wrong labels.

## Data sources

The nomenclatures come from open government data. All of them require
attribution; it belongs in the model card and in the app, not only
here.

- Surnames, given names: Swiss Federal Statistical Office (BFS)
- Localities with postcodes, streets: Federal Office of Topography
  (swisstopo)
- Places of origin: Federal Office of Justice (BJ/EJPD), eCH-0135
- Company names: Zefix, Federal Commercial Registry Office (EHRA), via
  LINDAS. Terms of use: Provide-the-Source (free use, attribution
  required)
